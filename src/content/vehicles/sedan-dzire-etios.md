### Comfort & Agility for Small Groups
The standard Sedan category is the workhorse of private transport in Udaipur. Featuring reliable vehicles like the **Maruti Suzuki Swift Dzire** and **Toyota Etios**, this category offers an optimal balance of budget-friendly pricing and comfortable air-conditioned travel.

### Why Choose a Sedan?
Udaipur's historic old city around Lake Pichola, City Palace, and Jagdish Temple features narrow, winding streets. A sedan navigates these tight corners effortlessly where larger coaches and buses cannot enter.

* **Recommended Capacity:** Ideal for **1 to 3 adult passengers**. While the rear bench seats 3 adults, comfort is highest with 2 adults in the back and 1 in the front passenger seat.
* **Luggage Guidance:** The boot comfortably fits **2 standard medium check-in suitcases** plus 1 or 2 small soft backpacks or laptop bags. If you are 3 passengers carrying 3 heavy hard-shell check-in bags, we strongly recommend upgrading to an SUV.
