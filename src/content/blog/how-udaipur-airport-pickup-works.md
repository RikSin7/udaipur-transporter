Arriving at a new airport can be stressful, especially if you are traveling with family, heavy luggage, or arriving on a delayed evening flight. Maharana Pratap Airport (Dabok) is located approximately 22 kilometers east of Udaipur city center, meaning a drive to Lake Pichola or old city hotels typically takes between 35 and 50 minutes depending on city traffic.

To ensure a seamless transition from the baggage carousel to your hotel room, here is exactly how our private meet-and-greet airport pickup protocol operates.

### 1. Pre-Arrival Coordination and Driver Allocation
Unlike ride-hailing apps where you must search for a car and negotiate parking bay pickup coordinates after landing, our coordination begins before your flight departs:
* **Driver Assignment:** 3 to 4 hours prior to your scheduled landing, our dispatch team shares your assigned driver’s full name, direct mobile number, and vehicle registration number via WhatsApp and SMS.
* **Flight Tracking:** We actively monitor incoming flight status using real-time radar data. If your airline experiences a minor departure delay from Delhi, Mumbai, or Jaipur, we adjust the driver’s dispatch time automatically without requiring you to make frantic phone calls from the tarmac.

### 2. The Meet-and-Greet Protocol at Dabok Terminal
Maharana Pratap Airport has a single, well-organized arrival terminal. Once you collect your check-in baggage and walk through the final customs/security exit doors:
1. **Locating Your Placard:** Walk directly toward the visitor greeting barrier outside **Arrival Exit Gate 1**. Your driver will be standing in the front row holding a physical placard displaying your primary passenger name.
2. **Luggage Assistance:** Your driver will assist in escorting your luggage trolley to our designated premium parking zone, loading all bags securely into the vehicle boot.
3. **Zero Parking Surcharges:** Standard airport parking access fees for vehicle staging are handled directly by our team; you will not be asked for cash at the airport exit boom barriers.

### 3. Understanding Waiting Time Rules
We provide a **60-minute complimentary waiting window** calculated from the moment your flight wheels touch down on the runway. This provides ample time for standard baggage retrieval and restroom stops. 

If your baggage is accidentally misplaced by the airline or you are detained inside the terminal for longer than an hour, simply drop a quick text to your driver on WhatsApp. We hold the vehicle at the terminal, applying only a nominal, pre-approved hourly waiting fee if the delay extends significantly beyond normal operational buffers.
