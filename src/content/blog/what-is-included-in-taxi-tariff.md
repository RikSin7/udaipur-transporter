In the Indian tourist transport industry, pricing confusion usually stems from quotes that sound suspiciously cheap at first glance, only to be inflated later by unexpected "actuals" like highway toll plaza receipts, driver overnight food allowances, and inter-city state taxes.

At Udaipur Royal Transporter, we operate on a strict **Owner-Approved Fact Policy**. Before you confirm any trip, we break down exactly what your tariff covers and where third-party actuals apply. Here is how private taxi billing mathematically works across Rajasthan.

### 1. Base Fare vs. Third-Party Actuals
When you receive a quote for a local Udaipur rental or an outstation trip to Jodhpur or Mount Abu, costs are divided into two distinct operational categories:

| Billing Component | Who Pays | Operational Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle & Fuel** | **Included in Quote** | Covers vehicle wear-and-tear, commercial insurance, and all diesel/petrol consumed along the agreed itinerary routing. |
| **Driver Allowance (Bhatta)** | **Included in Quote** | A fixed daily stipend compensating the driver for their food and basic lodging during outstation multi-day trips. |
| **Highway Toll Taxes** | **Billed at Actuals** | FastTag charges deducted at NHAI toll plazas along highways (e.g., Udaipur to Jaipur NH-58). These are either pre-calculated into an "All-Inclusive" quote or paid transparently as actual electronic receipts. |
| **Monument & Hotel Parking** | **Billed at Actuals** | Parking fees charged by third-party management at tourist spots like City Palace, Kumbhalgarh Fort, or lakefront hotels. |

### 2. How Local City Packages are Calculated (8 Hrs / 80 Km)
For local city movement within Udaipur municipal limits, pricing is structured around standardized block rentals:
* **The Garage-to-Garage Rule:** In compliance with commercial transport norms in Rajasthan, kilometre and time tracking begins when the vehicle leaves our central city garage and terminates when it returns to the garage after dropping you off.
* **Overtime and Extra Mileage:** If an 8-Hour / 80-Kilometre sightseeing day extends into an evening dinner trip lasting 10 hours and covering 95 kilometres, the overage is billed strictly at pre-published unit rates (e.g., ₹150 per extra hour and ₹12 per extra kilometre).

### 3. Interstate Border Taxes
If your itinerary crosses out of Rajasthan into neighboring states—such as a transfer from Udaipur to Ahmedabad (Gujarat) or Agra (Uttar Pradesh)—commercial transport vehicles must pay commercial interstate entry taxes at the state border RTO checkposts. On one-way drops, these border taxes are clearly itemized in your written email or WhatsApp estimate prior to vehicle dispatch so you never encounter surprise cash requests on the highway.
