### 1. Information We Collect
When you submit a travel request or contact us via our website, we collect only the essential personal information required to plan and coordinate your private transport in Udaipur:
* **Contact Details:** Full name, mobile/WhatsApp phone number, and email address (if provided).
* **Itinerary Information:** Travel dates, pickup locations, drop-off destinations, flight/train numbers, passenger counts, and luggage volume.
* **Technical Data:** Standard server logs, IP addresses, and browser types for basic website security and spam prevention.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
Your personal data is collected and processed exclusively for the following operational purposes:
* Reviewing your travel itinerary and vehicle category preferences.
* Communicating owner-approved price quotes, tariff structures, and vehicle availability directly to you via Phone, WhatsApp, or Email.
* Allocating drivers and sharing driver contact details prior to your scheduled pickup.
* Resolving customer support inquiries and maintaining internal booking records.

### 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Recipients
**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or travel aggregators.** Your data is shared strictly on a need-to-know operational basis with:
* **Assigned Drivers:** We share your name, pickup location, travel time, and contact number with your allocated local driver to ensure a smooth meet-and-greet at the airport, railway station, or hotel.
* **Legal Authorities:** We may disclose information if required to do so by Indian law, law enforcement agencies, or local transport regulatory bodies.

### 4. Data Retention
We retain your inquiry and trip coordination details only for as long as necessary to fulfill the transport service, resolve any follow-up customer inquiries, and comply with standard Indian tax and business accounting regulations. Once retention is no longer necessary, data is archived or securely deleted from our active communication channels.

### 5. Your Rights and Contact Information
You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact data submitted through our website. To exercise these rights or ask questions about our data handling practices, reach out directly to our transport coordination team using the contact details listed on this website.
